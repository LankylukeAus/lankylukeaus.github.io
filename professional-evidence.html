07 · ethics and accountability

Professional practice statement

I conduct security testing only where I have clear authorisation and remain within the defined scope. I preserve evidence, document assumptions and limitations, and report findings accurately without overstating certainty or impact.

I treat confidentiality, privacy and operational safety as part of the technical work. Public material is sanitised so that it demonstrates capability without exposing employer information, personal data, production details or reusable secrets.

I translate security obligations and technical findings into practical controls, risk decisions and measurable evidence. Where a control cannot be implemented immediately, I document the exposure, compensating controls, responsible owner and review point.

I maintain competence through structured training, practical labs, technical reading, projects and reflection. Offensive work is used to understand adversary behaviour and improve defensive capability, detection coverage and remediation quality.
